Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Infinite Fusion Calculator (the “App”) is a fan-made companion app for the game Pokémon Infinite Fusion. This policy explains what data the App does and does not handle. In short: the App has no user accounts, shows no ads, and collects no personal information about you.

No accounts No ads No analytics No tracking No data sold or shared

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account or sign in, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information — no name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers for advertising. The App contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device so it can work well and offline:

  • Favourites — fusions or Pokémon you choose to save.
  • A local content cache — recently viewed fusion details, so they load quickly and remain available without a connection.
  • App preferences — such as your light/dark appearance.

This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and is removed when you delete the App or clear its data.

Network requests

To display fusion data and sprite images, the App fetches content from a public Pokémon Infinite Fusion community API and image CDN. Like any request over the internet, these requests necessarily reveal your device’s IP address and standard technical details (such as the type of request) to the server that provides the data. We do not control and are not responsible for the logging practices of those third-party servers, and the App sends them no personal information about you. No request includes anything that identifies you personally.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
