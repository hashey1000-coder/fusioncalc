Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The App has no sign-in and no accounts. Your favourites are saved privately on your own device.

Does it work offline?

The Pokédex and your favourites work offline, and fusions you have already viewed are cached so they open without a connection. Browsing new fusions, artists and tags needs an internet connection to fetch fresh data and images.

A fusion shows “no custom sprite yet”. Why?

Not every one of the hundreds of thousands of fusion combinations has a hand-made (custom) sprite. When one doesn’t, the App shows the auto-generated sprite, and if that isn’t available either, a simple placeholder — never a broken image.

Are the sprites yours?

No — sprites are created by the Pokémon Infinite Fusion community and are credited to their artists inside the App.

Is this an official Pokémon app?

No. It is a fan-made, unofficial companion app and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, or The Pokémon Company.
